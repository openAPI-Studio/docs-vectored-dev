Clock

Display configurable clock gadgets on your Confluence page. Choose between analog and digital styles with timezone support.

Screenshot placeholder

How to Use

  1. Type /Clock in the editor
  2. Choose style: Analog or Digital
  3. Configure timezone and appearance
  4. Click Save — live clock displays on the page
