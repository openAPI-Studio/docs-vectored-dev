Typewriter

Animate text with a ChatGPT-style typing effect when the page loads. Multiple animation styles and visual themes for engaging content reveals.

Screenshot placeholder

How to Use

  1. Type /Typewriter in the editor
  2. Choose render style: Typewriter, Word by word, Line by line, or Fade in
  3. Choose speed: Fast, Medium, Slow, or Very Slow
  4. Choose a theme: None, Terminal, Retro Monitor, Matrix, Paper, Blueprint, or Hacker
  5. Toggle blinking cursor and line numbers
  6. Enter content and click Save
