Spoiler / Reveal

Hide content behind a pattern with an animated reveal on click. Perfect for quizzes, surprises, or progressive disclosure.

How to Use

  1. Type /Spoiler in the editor
  2. Enter the hidden content
  3. Configure the reveal pattern and animation
  4. Click Save — readers click to reveal the content
