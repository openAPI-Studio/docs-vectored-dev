Alerts and the notifications page

The bell, what the Wiki alerts you about, and the notifications page

1. The bell

The bell at the top right shows how many unread alerts you have (99+ past 99). Click it to open the panel; on a phone it fills the screen. New alerts arrive while you work, with no need to reload.

  • Unread and All tabs. Alerts are grouped by Today, Yesterday and Earlier.
  • Click an alert, or the dot beside it, to mark it read; click the dot again to mark it unread.
  • Open the page (or Open the question) goes to what the alert is about.
  • Mark all read marks every alert read. The settings icon opens your notification preferences.
  • View all notifications opens the notifications page.

Each alert is for you alone and names nobody else it went to. A newer alert about the same page and the same thing replaces the older one, so a busy page doesn't fill your bell.

3. The notifications page

View all notifications (or the address /alerts) lists every alert you have. It opens on Unread.

  • Show: Unread, All or Cleared, with how many each holds.
  • Severity and Category narrow the list. The Wiki's own alerts are info.
  • Select several to Mark read or Clear them together; on Cleared, Restore brings them back.
  • Clearing only hides the alert for you. A cleared alert shows Undo for a moment.
  • Preferences opens your notification preferences; Load more shows older alerts.

An alert's category on this page is technical (it names the page it is about). To stop alerts about a page, use Stop watching on the page rather than muting its category.
