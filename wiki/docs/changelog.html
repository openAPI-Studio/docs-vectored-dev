2026-11-21EditorFixed: after adding an image or another whole block to a column, clicking into another column left the block selected, so the next thing typed replaced it.

If your reverse proxy runs on another host and isn't listed, add it: the system check's new Forwarded headers line names an address that was refused.
2026-11-20PerformancePages are much smaller: each one carried every text of the screen's area again, and now the browser loads those once and keeps them.

2026-11-19TemplatesA template library under Settings → Templates that every space starts pages from, with twelve ready-made templates; a template can create a whole tree of pages; spaces can switch library templates off or keep their own version; and templates can be written in a template editor.

2. Initial release

The first release of the Wiki: a self-hosted team wiki that runs on a single server or as a cluster, with the same features either way. Later releases are listed above this one as they ship.

AreaWhat's included
SpacesSpaces that are private, internal or public, with roles for people and groups, a page tree, space settings, templates and a trash for deleted pages.
Pages and the editorA Word-like editor with headings and heading numbers, lists, tables, images with captions, videos and the "/" menu; blocks and macros such as panels, expands, decisions, excerpts and includes, page properties and reports, status, child pages, the page tree, recently updated and spreadsheet tables with formulas; labels and "Was this page helpful?"; copies and templates.
Writing togetherLive editing with everyone's cursors, autosave, drafts, publishing and scheduled publishing, review requests and review-by dates, versions you can compare and restore, comments, and watching and mentions.
Q&ACompany-wide questions and answers: votes, accepted answers, comments, follows, duplicates, mentions of people and pages, reputation, and public questions for anonymous readers; moderation.
CalendarsSpace calendars with events, leave and repeats, publishing plans and review dates, and a secret feed address for calendar apps.
AnalyticsPage, space and personal analytics and content health (stale pages, old drafts, broken links, disliked pages, searches that find nothing), with CSV export (Pro).
Moving contentImport Markdown files or a space from Confluence, mirror a docs repository, and export pages as Word or PDF.
Readers outside the companyAnonymous access to public spaces and public questions, and robots.txt for search engines.
PlatformEverything the Wiki shares with Depotex: users and directories, single sign-on, API tokens, OAuth applications, rate limits, policies, automation on events, plugins (including plugin blocks in pages), the audit log, personal data export and erasure, backups, the system check, monitoring, email and branding.
InstallationA setup wizard or an unattended setup file; a single server or a cluster; storage on a local disk, S3, MinIO or Azure Blob; recovery mode when an installed server can't start; phones and tablets supported throughout (Mobile).
