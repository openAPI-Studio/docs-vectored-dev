Known issues

Limitations and known problems in the current release, with workarounds

What doesn't work yet, or works with a catch, in the current release, and what to do meanwhile. Fixed issues leave this list and appear in the changelog.

Last reviewed: 21 November 2026.

1. Installation

IssueWorkaround
No container images or Helm chart yetRun the server and web app as services, or in containers you build
Live editing needs /api/ws routed to the server; without it the editor can't connectRoute it (see Install the Wiki)
The Wiki can't be served under a pathGive it its own host name
Some platform screens still name Depotex (for example Settings → General's address field, and the anonymous access text about downloads)—

2. Spaces, pages and media

IssueWorkaround
No per-page view or edit restrictionsPut pages that need other access in their own space
Nothing stops an admin from removing a space's last adminA super admin adds an admin back
No per-space attachment quota, no virus scan of attached files, no "Download all" and no previews of Office files (they download)Keep the blocked extensions for programs
Attachments lists and galleries show only the files of their own page; an old version's list shows today's files—
Tasks can't be ticked from a task report, and blog posts' tasks aren't in reportsOpen the task's page
A link card's text isn't refreshed after it is saved; previews read UTF-8 pages onlyChange the card and fill it in again, or type the text
Videos are played as uploaded: no transcodingUpload MP4 (H.264) or WebM that browsers play
PDF export draws Latin, Greek and Cyrillic text onlyExport to Word for other scripts
A scheduled publish runs up to a minute late, and any edit after scheduling cancels itSchedule again after editing
Review-by reminders go only to whoever set the date, as an in-app alert—
Tablets get the compact desktop sizes of controls—

3. Search, imports and Q&A

IssueWorkaround
Search finds page text only in the newest published version, not in drafts, earlier versions or attached filesOpen the page's history or the attachment
Imports: Confluence XML exports and Markdown only; restrictions aren't imported, and lists with filters the Wiki doesn't have are widenedRecreate restrictions as separate spaces; check the lists the report counts
Q&A, comment and mention alerts are in-app only, with no email—
Signed-out visitors can't use search or label pages in the web appLink to pages directly
No sitemap; robots.txt lists at most 1,000 public spaces—

4. Analytics and calendars

IssueWorkaround
Analytics retention can't be changed, and recording who read what can't be turned offSee Analytics for what is kept
Analytics days are UTC days—
Calendar feeds send at most 2,000 entries over a year and a month; repeats can't have exceptions—
A calendar feed's secret is in its address, which a proxy may logLeave queries out of the proxy log for /api/calendar/ical

5. Kee

IssueWorkaround
Ask Kee doesn't read blog posts or Q&A yet. Of attached files it reads PDF text, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) and plain text only: not presentations, scans, archives, old .doc or .xls, or password-protected files. Images are read only when you add them to a questionOpen them yourself
Excel values are read as stored: dates show as numbers, and a formula shows its last saved result, or nothing when the file holds noneAsk about the columns, or save the workbook again in Excel
Search by meaning compares every passage a person may read, with no vector index, so it slows down on very large Wikis. It needs the vector extension in Postgres, and a server notices it only after a restartUse the budget on Settings → Kee → Search, or leave it off
The switch on the Search tab is the same as Search by meaning on the Features tab; the other tab shows the old state until the page is reloadedReload the page
Questions you ask Kee are matched by meaning under the scheduled budget, not your own daily limitNone needed
The weekly space digest is shown only on the space's overview, with no email, and is written in the language of whoever turned it onOpen the space's overview
Merging a duplicate page brings over the other page's text only: its images, mentions and embedded items stay on the old pageCopy them by hand before moving the old page to the trash
The duplicate warning isn't shown for pages made by Ask Kee or an outside AI app, and never for pages under a page lockNone needed
Label suggestions offer new words, but know only the labels already used in the spaceAdd other labels by hand
A suggested alt text can be longer than the 150 characters asked forShorten it before choosing Use these
Outside AI apps with the administer scope list the administration tools to people who lack the permission; each call is refusedNone needed
Kee proposes changes to pages, comments and labels only; it can't move, archive, delete or restore pages, or change spaces, blog posts or questions. A whole-page change shows the whole page, without folding unchanged partsMake those changes yourself
A proposal is refused as Page changed when the page's draft holds edits it hasn't seen, even small onesAsk again on the page as it is now
In the unlikely case a server stops between writing a new page or comment from a proposal and marking it applied, applying it again later can make it twiceDelete the copy
The "with Kee" mark on a version names the model, not the agent used. A picked agent isn't remembered across page loads. Reassigning an agent to someone other than yourself has no screen (only Take over)None needed
A space can be given an agent for use only, not to editShare with a group instead
Outside AI apps: ending a connection blocks it but doesn't remove the person's sign-in or key, and can't be undone; the call limit and write budgets are counted per server without Redis; usage and the connections list cover the whole installation; each AI app is registered by an administratorThe person removes the connection and signs in again
The Outside AI apps tab edits who may connect by role onlyAdd groups in the Kee permissions grid on Features and access
Scheduled help: the findings' Update, Archive and Review are advice only, not actions; suggestions for questions match words in page titles only; the scheduled budget sends no alert at 80% or 100%Act on the page yourself; check the card on Features and access
Show me walkthroughs can't point at the controls of the Wiki's own settings pages, of Kee's Search tab, or of several space settings tabs (trash, import, locked pages, analytics, blog) yet: their how-tos are shown as textFollow the steps in these docs
Some screens of outside AI apps say "the assistant" or "AI" where they mean KeeNone needed
No screen for how long Ask Kee conversations are kept (30 days by default), or for the feedback people give on answersUse the API: GET/PUT /api/wiki/ai-settings (Manage Kee settings) and GET /api/ai/feedback/summary (See usage)
While an answer or summary is being written, Markdown marks such as ** show as typed; the formatted text replaces them when it is doneNone needed
The Ask Kee panel lies over the right side of the page instead of narrowing itClose it to read the page
In the editor, the selection's Kee button can lie over the line below the selection, and the panel for a selection opens under the whole blockNone needed
A version is marked "with Kee" by time: a result applied and then removed by hand still marks the next versionNone
Write the page is offered only on an empty page, and reads no other pages or filesWrite a short request with what the page needs
The Bedrock model list doesn't show the regions of each inference profile, and adding a provider doesn't list its models in one stepAdd models after saving the provider
Some notices say "AI" instead of "Kee" (the daily limit, provider and budget messages, the system check and alerts)None needed

6. Whiteboards

IssueWorkaround
The read-only view and whiteboard blocks refresh every 30 seconds, not liveOpen the whiteboard to see it live
Private mode hides notes on screen, in downloads and in search, but their text reaches the browsers of everyone who can draw on the board; votes aren't secret eitherDon't use private mode for confidential input
A template or copy made while private mode is on gets the hidden notes as blank notesReveal the notes first
Starting a timer or a vote isn't in the audit log and raises no event—
Adding a node to a mind map lays out the whole map again, moving nodes placed by handUndo the addition to put them back
Word and PDF downloads of a page show a whiteboard block as a link, not its pictureDownload the whiteboard itself as SVG or PDF
The PDF download doesn't turn rotated text, and wraps text a little differently from the boardUse the SVG download
Copies of a whiteboard stay in its spaceCopy its items and paste them into a new whiteboard in the other space
