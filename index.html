BYOAI* — Bring Your Own AI. These products work with an AI provider you already use: plug in your own API key (or connect your own AI assistant, in API Studio's case) and AI requests go to that provider under your own account and terms. You choose the provider, and usage is billed by them — never through a Vectored plan. Tiles marked Supports AI have AI features; Recognition Hub for Confluence uses Atlassian-hosted AI, so no key is needed there.
