How It Works

  1. Open Recognition Hub from Confluence or Jira global navigation
  2. Click + Give recognition and pick a card template
  3. Or describe the occasion and let AI draft the title, message, value and GIF for you
  4. Search teammates, write your kudos, tag a company value, add a GIF
  5. Post — the card lands on the live mural and recipients get a rich email
  6. The team reacts with emoji and comments; share cards by link, email, or page macro
