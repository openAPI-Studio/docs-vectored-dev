Rewards Setup (Tremendous)

Create a Tremendous account, build a campaign, get an API key, and connect it under Settings

Rewards let anyone giving recognition attach real money to it — the recipient picks a gift card or cash-out option and claims it. Recognition Hub does not handle the money itself: you open your own Tremendous account, you fund it, and Recognition Hub issues rewards against your balance. Nothing is charged by us, and rewards stay switched off until you complete the steps below.

Rewards are optional. Recognition Hub is free and fully functional without them — feed, GIFs, values, badges, AI assist and email all work with no Tremendous account. Skip this page entirely if you only want recognition.

Before You Start

  • USD only. If your workspace currency is anything else, reward features stay hidden. Set the workspace currency to USD first.
  • You need Confluence or Jira admin to reach Recognition Hub's settings.
  • Budget for real money. A funded Tremendous balance is what rewards are paid from. Start in sandbox if you just want to see how it works.
  • Talk to payroll first. Gift cards to employees are usually taxable compensation in most countries. Recognition Hub does not withhold or report tax — see Tax.

Step 1 — Create a Tremendous Account

  1. Go to tremendous.com and sign up. Tremendous is free to open — you pay only for the rewards you send.
  2. Complete their business verification. This can take a day or two, so start it early if you have a launch date in mind.
  3. Fund your balance under Billing → Payment Methods. Recognition Hub allocates from this balance; it can never spend more than what is there.

Want to try it without spending anything? Tremendous runs a separate sandbox at testflight.tremendous.com with its own login, its own API key and fake money. Everything below works identically there. Sandbox and production keys are not interchangeable — a sandbox key with the sandbox toggle off will be rejected.

Step 2 — Create a Campaign

A campaign in Tremendous is the menu of options a recipient sees when they claim a reward — which gift-card brands, which cash-out methods, and the branding on the claim page. Recognition Hub cannot issue a reward without one, so this step is not optional.

  1. In the Tremendous dashboard, open Campaigns and create a new one.
  2. Give it a name you will recognise in a dropdown later — Employee Recognition beats Campaign 1.
  3. Pick the reward options to offer. A broad mix (major retailers, prepaid cards, PayPal where available) means people actually want what they get. A narrow list is easier to defend to finance.
  4. Add your logo and a short message if you want the claim page branded.
  5. Save it. If you are using sandbox, create the campaign in the sandbox dashboard — campaigns do not cross over.

Step 3 — Get Your API Key

  1. In Tremendous, go to Settings → Developers → API keys (sandbox has the same page at testflight.tremendous.com).
  2. Create a key and copy it immediately — Tremendous shows the full value once.
  3. Paste it straight into Recognition Hub in the next step. Do not email it, put it in a ticket, or drop it in a chat channel: this key can spend your balance.

Recognition Hub stores the key in Forge encrypted storage. It is never written to the database, never returned to the browser, and never shown again after saving — the settings screen only ever displays connection status. Only the app's backend ever talks to Tremendous.

Step 4 — Connect It in Recognition Hub

Open Settings > Integrations and expand the Rewards — Tremendous panel.

  1. Paste the key into API key.
  2. Set the Sandbox mode checkbox to match the key you pasted. This is the single most common setup mistake — a mismatch gives you "Tremendous rejected this API key" even though the key is perfectly valid.
  3. Click Save key.
  4. Click Test connection. This verifies the key, pulls your live Tremendous balance, loads your campaigns, and registers a status webhook so redemptions update themselves.
  5. Pick your campaign under Reward catalog. If the dropdown is empty, go back to Step 2 — no campaign means no redemptions.

You should now see a green status, your Tremendous balance, and Status webhook: Registered. If the webhook says "Not registered", run Test connection again.

Step 5 — Create a Reward Pool

Connecting Tremendous does not yet let anyone give a reward. Money flows through pools: you allocate part of your Tremendous balance into a named pool, then assign people to it. Only someone assigned to a pool can attach a reward, and they can never spend more than that pool holds — which is how a recognition app avoids handing your whole balance to whoever clicks fastest.

Go to Settings > Reward Pools:

  1. Create pool — name it after the budget it represents, e.g. Engineering Q3.
  2. Load money — move an amount from your Tremendous balance into the pool.
  3. Assign — add the users or groups who may give from it. Assigning a group is the practical choice; one person belongs to one pool.

Every load, grant and adjustment is written to an append-only ledger, visible under Settings > Audit Log.

Step 6 — Check It End to End

  1. As someone assigned to a pool, post a recognition and attach a small reward.
  2. As the recipient, open My rewards in the hub. The balance should be there.
  3. Redeem it. Recognition Hub places the order with Tremendous, shows the claim link, and emails it too if email is configured.
  4. Open the link. You should land on your campaign's claim page with the options you chose in Step 2.

Do this once in sandbox before going live. When you switch, create a production key and campaign, paste the new key, and untick Sandbox mode.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat it usually means
"Tremendous rejected this API key"The Sandbox toggle does not match the key. Check it before assuming the key is bad.
"No reward catalogs found"No campaign exists on that account, or you created it in the other environment. Create one, then Test connection again.
Reward features are invisibleWorkspace currency is not USD.
"You are not assigned to a reward pool"Expected. Assign that person or their group to a pool in Step 5.
"Not enough balance left in your reward pool"The pool is empty, not your Tremendous account. Load more into the pool.
Redemption stuck as pendingA missed webhook. A background job reconciles hourly, so it normally clears itself; run Test connection to re-register the webhook.
Balance looks staleIt is cached from the last connection test. Run Test connection to refresh.

Tax and Money Handling

Gift cards to employees are almost always taxable compensation. In the US they do not qualify as a de minimis fringe benefit at any amount and are generally treated as wages; most other countries treat them as a benefit in kind. Recognition Hub does not calculate, withhold or report tax and has no payroll integration. It does give you the records: per-person reward totals under Settings > Analytics, and a per-person export under Settings > Privacy & Data. Agree the treatment with payroll before you launch, not after the first quarter closes.

On how the money is held: Recognition Hub never holds, receives or transmits funds. Your Tremendous account holds the balance and Tremendous issues the cards. The balances shown inside the app are an internal record of entitlement under your own recognition programme — not money, not transferable between people, and forfeited when someone leaves or the programme ends. Say so in whatever you publish to your staff.

When a person redeems, their name and work email address are sent to Tremendous so the card can be delivered. That is the only time employee data reaches them, and it only happens if you enable rewards.

Turning Rewards Off

Click Disconnect in the Tremendous panel. The key and webhook secret are deleted from encrypted storage and rewards stop immediately. Pools, balances and history are kept — saving a key again picks up exactly where you left off. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, including the credentials.
