Reactions & Comments

Celebrate posts with emoji reactions, confetti, and comments

Reactions

Every card shows three quick reactions (🎉 👏 ❤️) plus a + button that opens a 16-emoji palette. Reactions are persisted, counted across the team, and toggling one fires a confetti burst contained inside the card. Counts update live for everyone via the feed's 30-second refresh.

Comments

Add short congratulation comments under any card. Comments are persisted, show the author's name and avatar, and are included in the live refresh so conversations stay current without reloading.

Live Updates

The mural polls every 30 seconds (pausing while the tab is hidden) and offers a manual refresh button next to the “Updated just now” indicator. New cards slide in with an entrance animation; existing cards update reaction and comment counts in place.
