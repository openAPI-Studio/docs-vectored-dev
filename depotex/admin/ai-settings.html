AI settings

Settings → AI: the platform's model provider settings, and why Depotex doesn't use them yet

Settings → AI (marked New) is where the platform connects an organisation's own model provider account: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Amazon Bedrock. It is shown to super admins (Manage AI settings, ai:manage, is theirs only: providers, models and the budget cover the whole installation) on a Pro licence or higher.

Depotex has no AI features of its own yet. The page says so under What people get once it is on, and turning AI on makes nothing appear for anyone. Leave it off: while it is off, no text leaves the server.

1. What the page holds

TabWhat it configures
Providers and modelsProvider accounts (keys are encrypted on the server and never shown again), the models people may use, the default and the fallback
Features and accessA switch per feature (none in Depotex yet), and a permissions grid: Use AI per role, plus groups (See usage and Manage AI settings stay with super admins)
Limits and usageRequests per person per day and per minute, a monthly budget, and a usage report
Data handlingWhat would be sent to the provider, the providers' own terms, how long usage records are kept

The switch at the top right turns AI on or off for the whole installation. Off, every AI route answers as if it didn't exist and nothing is sent anywhere.

2. Events

Changes on this page raise ai.provider.*, ai.model.*, ai.settings.update and ai.permissions.update, which policies can refuse and which are in the audit log. See Events.
