Dashboard

Manage all your forms from the Forms & Frontdoor dashboard — search, sort, and take actions at a glance.

Search & Sort

Use the search bar to find forms by name. Sort by newest, oldest, alphabetical, or most responses. The header summarises your library — e.g. "5 forms · 3 owned · 2 shared with you".

Form Cards

Forms are displayed in a card grid. Each card shows at a glance:

  • An accent color strip matching the form's theme
  • An Owner or Editor badge — your role on that form
  • The form description
  • Stats: response count, field count, and how many people have edit access
  • Created date and time of the last response

Quick Actions

Each form card provides quick actions:

  • Edit — open the form builder
  • Share — direct link, embed instructions, and form ID
  • Duplicate — clone the form (you become the copy's owner)
  • Results — responses table, analytics dashboard, and automation logs
  • Delete — owner only; removes the form and all responses after confirmation
