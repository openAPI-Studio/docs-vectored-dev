Plans

What each plan includes, and what happens if one lapses

Draft. Plan names and prices are still being finalised with monday.com. Treat the figures below as placeholders until the listing is live.

Free

Up to 20 users. The recognition feed, posting, reactions and comments, company values, guidelines, badges, GIFs, email notifications, moderation and analytics — the whole product except rewards.

Premium

Everything in Free, for accounts above 20 users. Priced per seat and billed by monday.com.

Enterprise

Everything in Premium, plus rewards — funding reward pools, connecting Tremendous, and attaching real money to a recognition. See Rewards & Tremendous Setup.

Billing

Plans are bought and billed through monday.com, from Settings > Integrations or the app's marketplace listing. Recognition Hub never asks for payment details itself.

If a plan lapses

Nothing you already have is taken away. The feed, every past recognition, reward balances, redemption history and data exports all stay available. What stops is the paid action — granting new rewards — and a recognition with a reward attached still posts, without the reward. Money already funded into a pool remains yours to redeem.
